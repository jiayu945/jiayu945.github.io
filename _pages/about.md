Hi! This is Jiayu Zheng (in Chinese 郑家裕). I am a PhD student in the Chair of Landscape Planning at [TU Dortmund University](https://www.tu-dortmund.de/), advised by Prof. Dr. Aude Zingraff-Hamed. 

My research interests lie in the intersection of **spatial planning**, **nature-based solutions**, and **GeoAI**. My research aims to empower the planning and design of nature-based solutions with trustworthy GeoAI for sustainable and resilient urban futures.
